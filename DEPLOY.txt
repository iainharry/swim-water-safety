4. WHAT CHANGED - from the National Drowning Report 2026
   - National fatal drowning rate 1.31 -> 1.13 per 100,000 (2025/26).
   - 313 deaths nationally, 10% above the ten-year average of 284.
   - Born overseas 32% -> 39% (from 61% of known cases, up from 53%).
   - Location split added: coastal 151 (48%), inland 90 (29%),
     pools 41 (13%).
   - Dependent arithmetic recalculated throughout.

5. WHAT CHANGED - from the Aquatic Injury Prevention Agenda 2026-27
   - Cultural considerations: now carries the finding that 86% of
     surveyed international students misread the 'swim between the
     flags' symbol. This reframes multicultural over-representation as
     a signage design failure rather than a knowledge gap in the
     affected communities. Read that section before teaching it.
   - Year 9-10 workbook: 46% multicultural share among 15-24 year olds
     added to social determinants; the 67% alcohol-related reduction
     and 63% pool fencing reduction added to prevention strategies.
   - Teacher professional learning: research on why awareness campaigns
     under-perform (optimism bias, masculine norms, peer dynamics).
   - Debate framework: two new Year 9-10 motions on alcohol advertising
     and signage redesign. Now 8 motions at that level.

6. ONE FIGURE TO HANDLE CAREFULLY
   The Aquatic Injury Prevention Agenda reports 35 Victorian drowning
   deaths. That is YEAR TO DATE - eight months, 1 Jul 2025 to 28 Feb
   2026. The Victorian Drowning Report's 52 is a full financial year.
   They cannot be compared directly; doing so invents a 33% decline.
   Section 6 of the new brief teaches this as a data-literacy task.
   See DATA_PROVENANCE.md section 2b.
